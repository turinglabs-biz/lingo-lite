# 01 Ignored

Status: done
Blocked by: none

## What
All of the spec in one ticket: the learner marks Expressions Ignored, and practice leaves them out.

- **The mark:** a toggle beside the Focus pin at first exposure, on every question (before and after answering) and in the Phrasebook list. Stored in Progress (Dexie v5), cleared by Reset Progress.
- **Practice:** never in a Batch, Review session or Focus session; never an option; skipped for the rest of a session once ignored.
- **Counts and Stars:** left out of Home, Topics and Stats counts and of the Topic's Stars (recomputed on change).
- **Phrasebook:** an Ignored row is dimmed, never hidden.
- **Focus:** ignoring removes Focus; Focus removes Ignored.
