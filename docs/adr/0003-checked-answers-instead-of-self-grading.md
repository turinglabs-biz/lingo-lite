# Checked multiple-choice answers instead of self-grading

Status: accepted. It supersedes the self-grading part of ADR 0002.

Every question is now checked by the app. Speak asks the learner to say the Korean aloud, then pick it from four Korean options. Listen asks them to pick the English from four options. See asks them to pick the Korean for an Illustration. The learner did not want the app to rely on them grading themselves. Self-grades are unverifiable and prone to overconfidence (Dunlosky & Rawson 2012).

The cost is that picking from options is recognition, which the research finds weaker for retention than free recall (Rowland 2014; Kang et al. 2007). Speak keeps part of the recall benefit: the options stay hidden until the learner has said the answer and tapped "I said it". The wrong options come from the same Topic and never include Expressions that share the answer's Illustration, because those would also be correct. The FSRS grade is binary: right = Good, wrong = Again.
