A few minutes a day, then you're ready

  1. Meet 15 at a time

    Each new phrase arrives with its audio, spelled syllable by syllable the way it sounds. Hear it, say it out loud, then pick what it means.

  2. Say it, then check

    You see the English, say the phrase, then pick it from four. The app checks every answer, so you never have to grade yourself.

  3. Back just before you forget

    Spaced reviews bring each phrase back right before it would slip away. Misses come back sooner, easy ones later.

  4. Ready when you land

    Search the phrasebook offline, play any phrase slowly, or show it in big letters to the person in front of you.
